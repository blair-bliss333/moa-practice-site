Greenwood Village

Marjorie Park

MOA's sculpture garden, home of the Alice in Wonderland bronzes and the Cabinet of Curiosities and Impossibilities. The park has no regular daily hours. It opens for events, scheduled tours and SCFD Free Days, and to members by appointment. Concert ticket holders at Fiddler's Green Amphitheatre can walk in once the venue's doors open.

  • Address6331 S. Fiddler's Green Circle, Greenwood Village, CO 80111 · Directions
  • ParkingFree street and lot parking. During amphitheatre concerts, street parking is limited and paid lots are open.
  • Light railArapahoe Light Rail Station
  • AccessWheelchair accessible. Strollers welcome.

2026 SCFD Free Days

Marjorie Park opens to everyone, free, 10am to 4pm. Thank you to SCFD. Registration required.

Art in public places

Much of the collection lives outside Marjorie Park, in spaces anyone can walk into, any hour of any day. No ticket, no appointment.

  • EnglewoodAbout 30 works around the Englewood Civic Center and South Broadway between Yale and Hampden. Englewood Light Rail Station. Free parking south of the Civic Center. A few pieces are indoors, open during building hours.
  • Greenwood PlazaSculpture throughout the business district around Fiddler's Green Circle.
  • Westlands Park5701 S. Quebec St., Greenwood Village. Dawn to dusk.
  • How long30 minutes to an hour in either city. Three to four hours to see it all.

Before you go

Good to know

Food and drink

Picnics are welcome in the Englewood spaces. Each Marjorie Park event says whether you can bring food. Outside alcohol isn't allowed in the park; most events sell drinks.

Nearby coffee

Mangia Bevi Cafe near Marjorie Park. Nixon's Coffee House near the Englewood Civic Center.

Photos

Yes, please. Tag us @outdoorarts.

Pets

Only leashed service animals in Marjorie Park. Local rules apply where the art is in public places.

Bags

There are no lockers. Please leave large bags in your car.

Accessibility

Marjorie Park is ADA accessible. The Englewood Civic Center has elevators at both entrances. MOA doesn't lend wheelchairs.

FAQs

Questions visitors ask

Is the Museum of Outdoor Arts free?

The art in public places and Madden Gallery are always free, and Marjorie Park is free on SCFD Free Days. Events and guided tours are ticketed.

When is Marjorie Park open?

During events, scheduled tours and SCFD Free Days, and to members by appointment.

Do you offer discounts?

Members get discounted rates and member-only deals. Seasonal offers go out in the newsletter and on Instagram.

Is there a cafe?

Not on site. Most Marjorie Park events have food trucks or concessions.

How do I show my art at MOA?

MOA offers outdoor exhibition opportunities by artist call or invitation.

How is MOA connected to Fiddler's Green Amphitheatre?

MOA owns Fiddler's Green Amphitheatre and runs it in partnership with its tenant, AEG. For concert questions call 303-220-7000.

Can I rent a venue?

Yes. Marjorie Park, Madden Gallery and the Cabinet of Curiosities host weddings, parties and corporate events. See venues.

Can I visit on a weekend?

Marjorie Park opens on weekends for events. Madden Gallery opens select Saturdays; call to confirm. The public art is always open.
