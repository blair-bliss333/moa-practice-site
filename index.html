Good to know

Questions visitors ask

All FAQs →
Is the Museum of Outdoor Arts free?

The art in public places and Madden Gallery are free, and Marjorie Park is free on SCFD Free Days. Events and guided tours are ticketed, and members tour free.

When is Marjorie Park open?

During events, scheduled tours and SCFD Free Days, and to members by appointment. It has no regular daily hours.

Where is MOA?

Madden Gallery and MOA headquarters are at 6363 S. Fiddler's Green Circle, Suite 110, Greenwood Village, CO 80111. Marjorie Park is next door at 6331 S. Fiddler's Green Circle.

How much is a tour?

Guided tours of Marjorie Park and the Cabinet of Curiosities are $20 for adults and $10 for youth 2 to 17. Members tour free. See all tour prices.

Can I rent Marjorie Park for an event?

Yes. Marjorie Park, Madden Gallery and the Cabinet of Curiosities are all available for weddings, private parties and corporate events.

Can I see the sculptures at night?

Yes. The works in public spaces across Englewood and Greenwood Village are outdoors and accessible 24/7.
