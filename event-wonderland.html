Down the rabbit hole we go

This year marks the 75th anniversary of Disney's Alice in Wonderland. For one night, Marjorie Park becomes Wonderland, and it goes well past an ordinary outdoor movie.

  • CroquetA round on the lawn
  • The sculpture parkWalk among the Alice in Wonderland bronzes
  • Cabinet of CuriositiesOddities, fairy tales, and the rabbit hole itself
  • Face paintingAnd other whimsical activities
  • Wonderland cocktailsThemed drinks for the grown-ups
  • The movieTim Burton's Alice in Wonderland under the stars

Double feature: choose your own adventure

While Tim Burton's version plays outdoors, the portable Cricket Cinema in the park shows Disney's original animated Alice in Wonderland at the same time. It's a good fit for younger children, or anyone who wants the nostalgia.

Follow the white rabbit into the speakeasy

The $25 Wonderland Speakeasy ticket adds an Alice-themed cocktail inside the Cabinet of Curiosities before the movie.

Costumes encouraged

Come as Alice, the Mad Hatter, the Queen of Hearts, the Cheshire Cat, or a wonderfully curious look of your own.

Good to know

Outside alcohol isn't allowed in Marjorie Park; drinks are for sale. Only leashed service animals. The park is wheelchair accessible. Dress warm: it's an October night in Colorado.
