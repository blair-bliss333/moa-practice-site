About the Diorama

This Victorian architectural element was used as a bus stop for many years. MOA converted it into an outdoor diorama in 2016.

Located just outside the fence of Marjorie Park at Fiddler’s Green Amphitheatre, it is a unique, creative exhibition space that features rotating window displays and art installations for all to enjoy. A new installation fills its windows each season.
