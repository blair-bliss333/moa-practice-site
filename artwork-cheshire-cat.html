About the work

Lewis Carroll published Alice's Adventures in Wonderland in 1865 and Through the Looking-Glass in 1871. Harry Marinsky's Alice series follows Sir John Tenniel's original illustrations, turning them into bronzes you can walk around.

Marinsky keeps his forms simple and lets the surface do the work. Some areas are etched with pattern to suggest fur, cloth and texture; others are left smooth. The Cheshire Cat is always smiling, and in the story it fades away until only the grin remains. Marinsky picked out that grin in gold.

Carroll was one of the first children's authors to write without a moral lesson attached. Marinsky's series carries that spirit into the garden.
